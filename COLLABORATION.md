# Collaborating on Niger Law

Thanks for being here. This project is early, open, and genuinely needs people who care.

## Where We Are

Niger Law is being actively built right now. We have the vision, the architecture,
and we've already started pulling together the right people.

**We're currently collaborating with a law student** who is helping us identify the
right legal situations to prioritize and source the actual Nigerian legal documents
the AI will be grounded in. That collaboration is shaping everything about V1.

We're open to more contributors — but we want the right ones, not just anyone.

---

## Who We're Looking For

### 🏛️ Law Students / Legal Researchers
This is the most critical gap right now.

We need people who:
- Understand Nigerian law and can identify what's most relevant to young people
- Can help source, clean, and annotate legal documents (Constitution, ACJA, Police Act, etc.)
- Can review AI responses for legal accuracy
- Can write plain-language summaries of rights by situation

### ⚙️ Developers
- **Frontend** — React, Tailwind, mobile-first design
- **Backend / AI** — RAG pipelines, vector databases, Next.js API routes
- **DevOps** — Supabase, Vercel, CI/CD

### ✍️ Writers / Communicators
- Plain-language legal explainers
- Pidgin English translations (planned for V2)
- Community outreach and education

---

## What We're Building First

V1 is focused on **young Nigerians** — students and people in their 20s.
We're prioritizing the situations they face most:

- Police encounters and unlawful detention
- Arrest rights
- Tenant rights (first-time renters)
- Workplace rights (interns, entry-level workers)
- Gender-based violence and harassment

We are not trying to boil the ocean. We're going deep on these five situations first,
validating the product, then scaling to more legal domains.

## Ground Rules

- This project is for the people. No gatekeeping, no elitism.
- Legal accuracy matters more than speed. We won't ship something that gives people
  wrong legal information.
- We give credit. Everyone who contributes meaningfully will be acknowledged.
- Respect the mission. This is civic infrastructure, not a portfolio project.
